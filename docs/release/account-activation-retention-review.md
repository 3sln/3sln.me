# Account activation and retention review

Independent review started 2026-10-13. Focused activation and paid-retention protection checks pass; lifecycle execution remains pending. This document does not record a release approval or successful production notification delivery.

The requested activation policy applies to new account setup. An account must complete its authenticator and recovery-file steps and establish either a verified associated email or an enabled, verified push channel before receiving ordinary account authority. Existing active accounts are grandfathered. Removing a notification channel after activation must not log out its owner or prevent recovery; an account without a verified usable notice channel must be retained rather than automatically deleted.

The final activation check belongs in the identity service before its active-status/session transaction. Setup endpoints need limited pending authority, exact origin checks and durable identity binding. Frontend permission flags, posted email addresses, invitation query parameters and successfully stored subscription objects are not verification. The current invitation flow does not supply a verified-email claim that can replace an email challenge.

Email setup uses a short-lived code delivered through the configured mail transport. Its durable challenge must bind the account, pending setup generation, normalized email and purpose. Confirmation consumes the challenge once and retains the repository's exclusive email association checks. Failed attempts and resends need bounded durable budgets; resending cannot provide unlimited guesses. Verification for notifications must not automatically enable email sign-in. A challenge belonging to another setup browser or a superseded recovery/setup generation must fail.

Push setup needs an actual encrypted delivery challenge to the submitted subscription and a matching acknowledgment bound to the pending identity and subscription. The challenge must not also be returned through the setup API. Existing provider-origin restrictions must remain exact, with no arbitrary endpoint or redirect following. The browser must present the visible notification required by its subscription contract. Persisting a subscription alone neither proves device possession nor enables notification preferences. Missing push configuration or denied browser permission requires a working email alternative.

Recovery and lost-response resumption need the same final gate, preserving a verified associated email where appropriate. Pending authority rotation invalidates unfinished notification challenges. Existing device-login and ordinary session routes must not create authority for an account that is still pending. The grandfathering decision means previously active accounts remain usable; it does not justify activating newly created accounts without the required proof.

## Retention boundaries found in the existing implementation

The running account alarm performs reversible cold shelving, retaining identity, recovery and billing metadata. The lifecycle helper only returns a maintenance plan; it does not execute automatic account deletion. Its older conservative timing defaults are not evidence that the user-facing inactivity schedule is deployed. A future destructive executor still requires final atomic activity, ownership, payment-history and notice checks, together with the separately specified permanent namespace policy.

The permanent paid-handle marker is populated lazily when handle eligibility is requested. Absence of that marker therefore cannot establish that an account has never paid. Automatic-deletion protection needs durable, conservative subscription/payment history that survives expiry, cancellation, a subsequent Free state, recovery and shelving. History must be retained in the same transaction that replaces its former billing state, rather than reconstructed from an earlier stale read. Uncertain legacy history or a provider outage must not become evidence that deletion is safe. This protection is separate from the positive-payment rule for earning a permanent paid handle.

Automatic identity deletion and explicit owner content deletion are distinct. Protected accounts may still be shelved, and owners may still remove their own objects. Expiring storage entitlement must pause new growth without deleting existing data.

The Cloudflare mail resource returns a provider acceptance identifier. That identifier is not an independently verified delivery receipt. Existing lifecycle email templates are resources for future callers; their presence does not establish a scheduled notice sender. Successful onboarding code confirmation proves access to that email at setup time, not delivery of a later deletion notice.

## Warning, suspension and deletion follow-up

The user subsequently requested a warning, suspension after a configured interval, and deletion after a further interval. This requires an executing durable state machine beyond the existing pure planner. The canonical account-local lifecycle record should carry its phase, generation, inactivity evidence, notification attempts and monotonic deadlines. Persisted alarms must resume bounded work after restarts; a failed notice cannot silently advance a deadline or become a successful notice on retry.

Payment protection applies before every automatic phase, including warning and suspension. A fresh owner or app activity event, reactivation or verified payment can invalidate an earlier generation. Transitions and final effect checks must use the same account-local authority as billing and resource activity. The separately sharded identity row alone cannot atomically fence already-issued contact, federation or resource capabilities. Mirrored identity status is not a substitute for authoritative checks at their effects.

Suspension retains cold data and blocks normal account and app operations, but must preserve restricted authentication, status, confirmation and reactivation. Reusing the existing inactive-account authentication rejection without an explicit restricted path would prevent owners from saving their accounts. Recovery remains available under its proof and setup requirements. Existing notification credentials, paid-protection history and reactivation information must survive cold shelving.

Before destructive cleanup, the executor needs a final activity/payment/phase-generation check and a fence against newly admitted normal effects. It must retain permanent username claims and must not delete objects in externally connected storage. Interrupted cleanup must be resumable without repeating unrelated effects. A storage archive or lifecycle status write is not itself proof that an account was deleted.

The user requests multiple notice attempts without a response, rather than proof that a person read each notice. The coordinator selected provider-accepted receipts on a previously verified channel as the evidence policy. This is an executable policy without inventing a client-acknowledgment requirement that would prevent cleanup of abandoned offline accounts. An acceptance is recorded as `acceptedAt`, never described as proof of delivery or awareness. The earlier suggestion of requiring independently confirmed delivery was a conservative design option, not an additional approval gate.

The recommended execution contract is at least three distinct, spaced scheduled attempts across each phase's grace interval, including at least one recorded acceptance for that phase before advancing. Retries of the same attempt must not count as three notices. Every receipt binds its unique attempt, lifecycle generation and current verified channel revision. The first accepted notice establishes a fixed phase deadline; failed retries neither fabricate success nor shorten it. All failed or indeterminate attempts, known bounces or suppression hold destructive progress. The final transition still checks activity, paid protection, phase generation and required attempt evidence. Exact intervals and implemented handling belong in the lifecycle specification and decision ledger; no notice scheduler or destructive executor is implied by these recommendations.
